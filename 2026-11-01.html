The Sunday Buzz

What went into the hive this week, and what's coming next.

Published Nov. 1, 2026 · Page look updated Nov. 2, 2026
Two days to Election Day. This week we published race files for SD-7, HD-6, HD-66, HD-13, UT-4, SD-21 and Wasatch County Council Seat C, explained how Public Infrastructure Districts work and who pays, set housing promises next to the record, and traced how one book list moved through Utah’s government. Each box below is one repo or project, and every item is tagged New or Updated.

🏠 PIDs and housing

  • New How PIDs Work: Who Pays, and a Fix Already Working in Utah: how Utah’s Public Infrastructure Districts start, grow and bill homeowners; what the SEC and the State Auditor said at the Capitol; what UIPA and MIDA told lawmakers, checked against the record; and a payoff-first rule already working in Utah. With a plain-words version.
  • New A Shortage of Homes, or a Shortage of Homes People Can Afford?: what leaders say about building more near transit, set next to the public record: prices, incomes, the bill text, and what Utah’s transit-zone projects delivered. With a plain-words version.
  • New Ogden and Weber County: Which Homes Are Short?: Weber County’s own housing data, prices against paychecks, what “affordable” means in Ogden’s new apartments, and where the area’s tax growth goes.
  • New Wildcat Square CRA: Ogden’s Redevelopment Agency plans to collect up to $3,330,116 in future taxes over 15 years to help build student housing on a Weber State practice field. Most of it comes from Ogden School District. The hearing is Nov. 17.
  • Updated The Hidden Bill (“Affordable” House) story, the PID mechanism map and the Nordic Village case file, with dated notes.

📚 Education and the Bill Tracker

  • New How a Book Gets Pulled From Every Utah School: how Utah’s statewide school book removal works, the bills behind it, the State School Board’s Aug. 6, 2026 library review vote, and a book-challenge leader’s political giving, from public records. With a plain-words version.
  • New Utah’s School Book Laws, 2022–2026: six Utah bills on sensitive material in school libraries, who sponsored them, every recorded vote, what passed and what died, graded by the collective rubric. With a plain-words version.

🗺️ Utah Maps

  • New MAP 011: How Close to the Lake?: the big West Weber and Plain City developments, how close they sit to the Great Salt Lake, state wildlife areas and Willard Bay, and which land is in a FEMA flood zone.
  • New MAP 010: The Half-Mile Around Every Station: a half-mile circle around all 65 FrontRunner and TRAX stations, the distance the federal Build HUBS Act (S. 3636) would use for cheaper federal loans.

🏜️ Public Lands and water

  • New Hansel Valley: What No One Studied: what the public record shows about Hansel Valley’s springs, faults, heat and weather as a data center and 7,500 MW power plant seek Salt Wells Spring water (a55167), and what no one has studied.
