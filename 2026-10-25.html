📅 Before you vote

  • Registration: the regular deadline was Oct. 23. You may still be able to register in person and vote a provisional ballot. Call your county clerk to confirm.
  • In-person voting in Weber County: Tuesday, Oct. 27 through Friday, Oct. 30, noon to 6 p.m., Weber Center basement, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden.
  • Election Day, Nov. 3: 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. at the Weber County Fairgrounds Exhibit Hall (1000 N. 1200 W., Ogden) and the Ogden Valley Branch Library (131 S. 7400 E., Huntsville). Drop boxes close at 8 p.m.
  • Mailing your ballot late? Use a drop box instead.
  • Weber County Elections: 801-399-8034. Other counties: vote.utah.gov.
